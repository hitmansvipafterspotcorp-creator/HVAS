How to play

The game: bingo, but the numbers are songs. The host calls songs from the deck one at a time. When a called song is on your card, tap it to mark it. A square only counts after its song has been called — the host's phone checks every card, so nobody can mark early or fake a win.

Rounds: Round 1 — first to complete 1 line wins (50 pts). Round 2 — 2 lines (100 pts). Round 3 — the full card (150 pts). Marks carry over between rounds.

Win shapes: the host can set any shape as a round's target — Line, Two Lines, X, L, T, U, Four Corners, Plus/Cross, Frame, or the Full Card. Your card shades the squares of the shape you're chasing, and a picked shape pays squares × 10 points.

The music: every called song plays out loud on the caller's phone — tap ▶ Play the song on yours to hear it too. Mic-square performances play the track for a full minute.

🎤 Mic squares: every card has 5 pink mic squares. When a mic song gets called, the player can claim the square — but it only counts if they perform the song LIVE, camera on every screen in the room, TikTok-style, while the track plays for a full minute. Claim it and perform, or pass — decline, and the square is gone.

⚔️ Battles: if the same called song is a mic square on two cards and both players claim it, it's a battle — both perform live, side by side, on the same song. The room votes for a winner, and the winner takes the square. The loser doesn't get it.

Voting: after each performance the room votes — 🔥 FIRE = 3 pts, 👏 SOLID = 2 pts, 😐 MID = 1 pt. In a battle the room picks a winner instead: every pick is worth 3 pts and the winner gets a 10 pt bonus. Performance points stack on the scoreboard all night.

Audience: late to the game? Join as audience with the room code — no card, but you watch the stage and your votes still count.

Ways to play: online rooms (share the 4-letter code), hotspot rooms (everyone joins the host's phone hotspot — no internet at all), or solo practice.

Decks: a deck is a night — pick the theme that fits your room, or build your own deck from the song pool.

Online rooms use the free PeerJS broker. Hotspot games pair phone-to-phone with QR codes — no internet, no servers, once everyone is on the host's hotspot.
